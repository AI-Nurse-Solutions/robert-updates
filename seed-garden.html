Artifact Lifecycle

Publication is not execution

A seed may stop at any stage. This separation is a safety feature, not friction.

Seed→Draft→Publish→Inspect→Fork→Test→Evaluate→Review→Approve→Authorize→Execute→Monitor→Improve→Retire
Development Path

From soil to forest

The capability network should earn execution authority progressively rather than assuming it.

0 · Soil

Define scope, EDENA tiers, prohibited data/actions, reviewer roles, threat models, incident and revocation process.

Question: Can we govern this?

1 · Seed Bank

Start with non-executable role profiles, prompt packs, rubrics, synthetic scenarios, guides, provenance and review status.

Question: Can we safely share intelligence?

2 · Greenhouse

Add signed low-risk tools, local automation blueprints, manifests, dependency scanning, regression testing, rollback and revocation.

Question: Can we safely execute it?

3 · Forest

Allow qualified institutions and professional groups to maintain governed collections while preserving separate trust domains.

Question: Can it federate without centralizing?
