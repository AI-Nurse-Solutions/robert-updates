# SS-02 — Make evidence inspectable

**Engineering prototype delivered/tested; independent review and human acceptance pending.**

## Human need and scope
A nurse can examine actual source passages, recognize limitations and distinguish a source quotation from AI-assisted interpretation or missing information. This extends the same SS-01 mission; it is not clinical guidance, a retrieval agent, live Hermes integration or an operational portfolio.

Primary pillar: Knowledge. Dependencies: Judgment for appraisal, Capability for bounded inspection, Contribution for attribution and version/rights records. Main risks: false assurance and reading burden. The inherited IoC source distinctions remain separate from this proposed implementation; private drafts and conflicting doctrine were not published or reconciled.

## Implementation
Three short inspected primary excerpts (NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2.5, W3C PROV-O 2013 section 3.1, and Buçinca et al. 2021 versioned author abstract) carry locators, versions, excerpt hashes, attributions, rights and inspection limits. Guidance, standard and research abstract are distinguished; the full study methods were not appraised.

Seven claim records span evidence, synthesis, inference and missing information. Six working glossary entries disclose pending review. A fixed build-time reader checks exact admitted pack/version/mission/scope before reading, then verifies returned bytes. This is not authenticated authorization or privacy classification.

Source cards and filters expose exact linked passages. Synthetic diagnostic copies flag missing references, changed revisions and changed hashes without modifying the original mission or corpus. Resolving a link never verifies semantic support; the software cannot establish that a linked interpretation is true. Quote matching is integrity only. No model, network reader, Hermes, cloud, portfolio or publishing operation is activated.
