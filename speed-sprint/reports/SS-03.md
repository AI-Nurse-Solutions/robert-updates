## Implemented behavior

Record an initial interpretation or explicit uncertainty before a separate on-demand reveal of a labeled prepared AI-assisted fixture. Accept, revise, reject and withhold are unselected alternatives. Reasons, alternatives and consequences are recorded; revision requires replacement wording; self-reflection is optional.

Case/evidence changes close the comparison without overwriting prior choices. Returning a selector does not silently reactivate the round. New comparison explicitly uses baseline evidence. Pause blocks further practice transitions; clear requires confirmation; history is bounded to twelve rounds.

The three exercises concern omission, competing considerations and unsupported certainty. They are synthetic nonclinical contexts, not patient cases, observed user feedback or live model responses. The session is `memory_only`, `not_authenticated`, and `no_execution_permission`; reload clears entries. The prepared proposal is inspectable in page source, so reveal order is an instructional interaction rather than a security boundary. Validation is not a sandbox or tamper-proof audit trail.
