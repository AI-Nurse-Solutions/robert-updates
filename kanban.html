How a thought grows

Seed→Germinating→Growing→Blooming→Harvested
  • Seed: captured without over-judging it.
  • Germinating: the idea is being framed, researched, or connected to doctrine.
  • Growing: there is an owner, next action, or experiment.
  • Blooming: it is in public use, pilot, partnership, submission, or deployment.
  • Harvested: a concrete outcome, artifact, lesson, or evidence has been produced.

Update rule

After a substantive discussion, new thoughts can be appended to assets/seeds.js. Existing seeds are promoted only when there is evidence of movement. No seed is deleted merely because it fails; failed experiments become harvested learning.

Doctrine: signal → attention → contribution → merge → advance. The board is a practical personal expression of the Civilization Layer's feedback-loop discipline.
