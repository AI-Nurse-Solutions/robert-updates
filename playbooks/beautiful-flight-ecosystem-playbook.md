# In Beautiful Flight + Nurse AI OS Ecosystem Playbook

**Working Draft 1.0 — August 2026**

> **Free access. Guided growth. Personalized support.**
>
> Meet people where they are. Help them take one safe, useful next step.

## Executive operating decision

- **In Beautiful Flight** is the shared discovery and trust bridge.
- **Patty Hsu Practice** remains Patty-owned and distinct.
- **Nurse AI OS setup, group pathways, workshops and implementation support** are a joint effort.
- **Robert's professional and Sutter-facing relationships** remain Robert-led.
- **Google Drive** is the draft library; **GitHub** is the approved source of truth.
- The initial offering is **non-PHI, non-clinical and human-controlled**.

## Ecosystem

```text
Audiences
  ↓
In Beautiful Flight — shared discovery, stories, demonstrations and Q&A
  ├─ Patty Hsu Practice — life coaching, speaking, personal transformation
  ├─ Nurse AI OS — free access, guided setup, group pathways, manager and department services
  └─ Robert Professional Network — Sutter relationships, governance, hackathon and conferences

  ↓
Feedback and learning loop
```

## Client journey

1. Human need: pain, aspiration, curiosity or skepticism.
2. Discovery: 90-second video, referral, conference or social post.
3. Trust: In Beautiful Flight, pre-recorded core plus live Q&A.
4. Free value: Nurse AI OS starter and one small safe win.
5. Guided activation: individual setup or small-group practice circle.
6. Branch: Patty coaching, manager support, department readiness or institutional pilot.
7. Evidence: results, corrections, repeat use, payment and referrals.

## Implementation sequence

### Days 0–30 — Prove the job
- Launch the minimum site family.
- Publish three short videos.
- Test the free starter and guided setup with 5–10 people.
- Deliver one manager or department workflow packet.

### Days 31–60 — Prove the habit
- Run the first practice circle.
- Improve the recorded path.
- Test a second use case with returning users.
- Publish the first case study.

### Days 61–90 — Prove economic value
- Sell a department readiness sprint.
- Secure one paid engagement or formal pilot commitment.
- Build the conference workshop kit.
- Convert successful workflows into reusable assets.

### 90+ days — Build the learning engine
- Governed capability library.
- Standard delivery and reviewer roles.
- Evidence-led HERMES integration.
- Institutionally authorized pilots only when controls exist.

## Core principles

- Lead with pain or aspiration, not AI vocabulary.
- Value first, vocabulary second.
- Support before product.
- Capability over dependency.
- Claims must never exceed evidence.
- One small win before expansion.
- Personal, community, professional, institutional and clinical trust domains remain separate.
- Agents propose. Humans judge. Nurses steward.

## September definition of done

A visitor can understand the ecosystem; choose a path; receive free value; request paid support; and complete one useful, governed, non-PHI outcome. The team can deliver that outcome without reinventing the process.
