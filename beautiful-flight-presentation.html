Personal possibilities

Not every use needs to be about work.

Plan

Weekly priorities, household projects, travel research, learning goals, and long-range plans.

Create

Writing, reflection, creative work, idea development, and personal knowledge organization.

Remember

Project history, decisions, important documents, and deliberately chosen context.

Reflect

Tradeoffs, assumptions, alternatives, and questions that still need human judgment.

AI can help organize options. It should not quietly become the decision-maker.08
Thinking partner, not answer machine

A useful AI should not merely tell you what you want to hear. It should help you see assumptions, alternatives, missing evidence, and uncertainty.

Ask what I already think Challenge the weak assumption Present the strongest counterargument Separate fact from inference Tell me what I must verify
Capability should grow without turning into dependency.09
Start with the boundary

A personal/community system is not a clinical deployment.

Appropriate starting point

Career, learning, writing, portfolio, business planning, synthetic examples, and non-PHI projects.

Requires institutional governance

Employer workflows, internal systems, confidential operational data, staffing, and department automation.

Do not place here

PHI, patient-specific diagnosis or treatment, medication decisions, unauthorized charting, or clinical actions.

Personal use, community experimentation, and institutional clinical authority are separate trust domains.10
Where does the intelligence run?
☁️

Cloud

Powerful frontier capability and convenience. Data leaves the device under provider controls.

💻

Local

User-controlled hardware, selected private context, offline potential, and direct maintenance responsibility.

🔀

Hybrid

Routine and sensitive context local; selected difficult tasks routed to approved cloud models.

The important capability is not choosing one forever. It is making the routing visible, proportional, and reversible.

Local-first does not mean local-only.11
Why consider local-first?

More control over the nurse’s private context.

Reduce unnecessary data transmission
Keep selected memory on the device
Continue some work offline
Use smaller models for routine tasks
Make permissions easier to inspect
Revoke tools and access directly
Reduce dependence on one provider
Choose when frontier capability is justified
Local-first can increase control; it does not eliminate governance obligations.12
What local does not solve

Local is not a magic privacy shield.

Device theft or compromise
Weak passwords or unencrypted storage
Malicious or vulnerable dependencies
Undisclosed network calls
Excessive file permissions
Poor update and backup practices
Model hallucination
Hardware and maintenance burden

Local can improve control. Security still has to be designed and maintained.

Ask: What data? What permissions? What network calls? What stop control?13

3

71%

The correct response is not “Trust the provider.” The correct response is: Show me the data boundary.

Pew Research Center, Americans and AI 202617
Show me the data boundary

Seven questions before a personal AI task begins

1. What data?
Exactly which files, preferences, or context?
2. Where is it stored?
Local drive, encrypted database, cloud, or several places?
3. What leaves the device?
Prompts, attachments, telemetry, or outputs?
4. What permissions?
Which folders, credentials, tools, and destinations?
5. What action can it take?
Draft only—or send, publish, purchase, or modify?
6. What is the stop path?
Pause, revoke, remove, or roll back?
7. Who is accountable?
Which human reviews the result and owns the decision?
Minimum data. Visible permission. Human confirmation. Clear stop path.18

Your turn

“I want my personal AI system to help me __________________.”

Choose one useful, non-PHI problem. Keep it small enough to test and clear enough to verify.

Start with one problem you understand—not a vague desire to “use AI.”25
Design your first governed use case

1. Purpose

What problem am I trying to solve?

2. Inputs

What approved, non-PHI information may it use?

3. AI role

Research, organize, summarize, challenge, draft, or track?

4. Prohibitions

What must it never access or do?

5. Human gate

What requires my explicit approval?

6. Definition of done

How will I verify that the result is useful and accurate enough?

A good first use case is bounded, reversible, observable, and verifiable.26
Starter specification
Help me with [defined non-PHI task] using only [approved sources or files]. You may [allowed actions]. You may not [prohibited actions]. Before [consequential step], stop and request my approval. Separate facts, assumptions, and recommendations. Identify uncertainty. I will verify the result using [criteria or source].
Specification is stronger than a vague prompt because it defines authority, boundaries, and verification.27
After the Flight · Q&A triage

Not every use case belongs in the same trust domain.

Personal / professional

Career, study, writing, portfolio, entrepreneurship, and non-PHI project organization.

Institutional

Employer workflows, confidential data, department automation, systems access, and formal deployment.

Clinical / prohibited here

Patient-specific diagnosis, medication, treatment, PHI processing, autonomous charting, or clinical action.

A personal installation does not confer institutional or clinical authority.28
Five principles to take with you
  1. Capability, not dependency.
  2. Local-first does not mean local-only.
  3. Show me the data boundary.
  4. Use the smallest sufficient intelligence.
  5. Agents propose. Humans judge. Nurses steward.
A personal AI system should amplify agency while preserving judgment, privacy, and accountability.29
